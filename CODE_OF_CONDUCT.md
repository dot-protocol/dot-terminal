# Community guidelines

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Critique designs and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of private data are
not acceptable. Respect participants' consent and boundaries.

Maintainers may edit/remove abusive content and restrict participation. Enforcement
should explain the behavior at issue and allow appeal to another repository
administrator where available. Report conduct concerns privately to repository
maintainers through an available private GitHub channel; do not publish private
information in a report. An independent reporting contact is a governance milestone.
